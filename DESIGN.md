# MediGem Soft Clinical Workstation

The product should read as a calm, premium clinical workstation: the page is the canvas, with hierarchy supplied by Switzer typography, alignment, whitespace, quiet rules, and restrained tinted regions rather than stacked cards.

## Foundations

- Typeface: Switzer from Fontshare, used everywhere including identifiers and clinical values.
- Canvas: `#F8F7F3`; primary surface: `#FCFBF8`; rules: `#DDDDE2`.
- Ink: `#202638`; secondary ink: `#667085`.
- Supporting surfaces only: lavender `#E9E8F2`, powder blue `#DCE7F0`, sage `#DDE9DF`, peach `#F3DDD4`.
- Periwinkle `#6676B8` is the product accent. Amber, coral, and green are reserved exclusively for clinical state.

## Type scale

- Patient name: 48px / 600
- Page heading: 28px / 600
- Section heading: 17px / 600
- Body: 15px / 400
- Clinical values: 24px / 600
- Metadata: 13px / 400

## Component rules

- Avoid enclosing ordinary sections in cards. Prefer a heading, whitespace, and one horizontal rule.
- Vitals are one horizontal row divided vertically, never individual tiles.
- A clinical assessment is one lavender-tinted region whose interior uses simple rules and rows.
- Emergency information uses a thin dusty-coral left rule with a muted coral wash; never a saturated banner.
- Navigation selection is a low-contrast lavender/periwinkle wash, not a pill.
