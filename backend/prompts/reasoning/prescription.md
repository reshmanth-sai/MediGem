# Prescription Document Verification Guidelines

1. Transcribe every medication on the document exactly as written (name, strength, dose, frequency, route, duration) as observations with source "prescription". This is verification of an existing order, not a recommendation, and is required.
2. Flag potential drug-drug interactions or contraindications with the reported patient symptoms or allergies as red_flags.
3. Do not add, remove, or alter any medication or dose. In recommended_next_step, direct the health worker to verify the transcription against the original and to route any change through the authorizing physician.
